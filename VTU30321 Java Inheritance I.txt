Java Inheritance I
https://www.hackerrank.com/challenges/java-inheritance-1/problem
Program:-
class Bird extends Animal{
    void fly(){
        System.out.println("I am flying");
    }

    void sing(){
        System.out.println("I am singing");
    }
}
Output:-
Your Output (stdout)
I am walking
I am flying
I am singing
Expected Output
I am walking
I am flying
I am singing
